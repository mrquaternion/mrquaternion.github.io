Atmosbios Lab

May 2025 — April 2026

At Atmosbios Lab, I built CarbonCast, a Python data pipeline that retrieves and processes Copernicus ERA5 climate reanalysis data for climate and ecosystem research.

I developed configuration-driven workflows for polygon aggregation and station-level extraction, NetCDF processing, AmeriFlux predictor preparation, and model-ready datasets, with command-line and compute-cluster support for reproducible analyses.

Projects

SportLink — Social Platform for Sports Activities

SportLink is a mobile application developed in SwiftUI with a Firebase backend. It is designed as a social platform to connect people through sports by making it easy to discover, organize, and join activities locally.

The app features an interactive map (Mapbox), personalized recommendations, availability-based matching, and social features such as profiles and activity history. The goal is to reduce friction in finding partners and building consistent sports habits.

Astro — Satellite Tracking and Space News

Astro is an iPhone and iPad app built in SwiftUI for exploring orbital assets, following satellite positions, and reading space-related news in one focused experience.

The app combines a map-first tracking interface, mission context, offline-ready features, and a clean information flow for people who want to keep space activity close.
